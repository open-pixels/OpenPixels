OpenPixels — free AI image upscaler, nothing uploaded

Right-click any image on any page and make it sharp. Upscale up to 8x,
clear noise and compression artefacts, rebuild blurred faces, colourise
black-and-white photographs, and remove backgrounds.

Everything is free. No account, no trial, no watermark, no credits, and no
limit on how many images you do.

WHY IT IS FREE
Your image is processed by your own device, inside your browser. There is no
server doing the work, so there is no per-image cost to pass on to you.
Tools that charge for this upload your photo and pay for the GPU that
handles it; that is what their subscription covers. There is no such bill
here, so there is nothing to charge you for.

NOTHING IS UPLOADED
Your image never leaves your browser. The only thing this extension
downloads is the AI models themselves, once, from openpixels.app — and after
that it works with no network at all. It asks for no access to any website
until you right-click an image on one, and then only for that site.

SEE WHAT CHANGED
A before/after slider, and real measurements underneath: how much sharper
the result is, how much noise came out, and whether it stayed faithful to
your original. No other tool in this category shows you either. That last
number matters — it is how you know the model enlarged what was there
instead of inventing something new.

WHAT IT DOES
• Upscale 2x, 4x or 8x, or clean up an image at its original size
• Remove noise, grain and JPEG compression artefacts
• Rebuild faces that have gone soft in scans and old photographs
• Colourise black-and-white photographs
• Remove or replace a background
• A separate model tuned for drawings and anime
• Process a whole folder at once, with no limit, and download a ZIP
• Works offline after the first use

WHAT IT DELIBERATELY DOES NOT DO
AI portrait generation — the feature that turns your photo into a stylised
avatar. It cannot run on your device, so it would need a server, a price and
an account. The products that offer it also draw steady complaints about
changing people's skin tone and features. Making your photo clearer and
generating a new one are different jobs, and this extension only does the
first.

OPEN SOURCE
All of it, including the exact steps that produced each model file and their
checksums, so you can rebuild it and compare. The models are Real-ESRGAN,
GFPGAN, DeOldify, U²-Net and YuNet, each under a licence that permits this
use.
